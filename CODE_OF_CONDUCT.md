# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being direct about technical disagreements and kind about people;
- accepting constructive criticism, and giving it about the work rather than the
  person;
- saying "I do not know" and "I was wrong" without drama;
- respecting that the maintainer's time is finite and unpaid.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information — a physical or e-mail address, and in
  this project specifically **any real customer, student or child record** —
  without explicit permission;
- pasting real personal data into an issue, a pull request or a test fixture.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the channel in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are reviewed and handled in a way that
protects the reporter's privacy.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not follow this
document, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Where a decision is made, the
reason will be stated.
